Question

Tell me about a time when you went to listen to some live music. Perhaps it was at a concert or a live cafe. What was the mood like and how did you like the music you listened there?

Answer

A couple of years ago, I remember going to a concert at the Olympic Stadium.
I went there with my friends. Since this was my first time, I was very curious and excited. It was very special because the singer is my favorite artist. Also, I never saw his performance in person. The concert began with his powerful dance.
I moved to the music while singing the lyrics. All the performers on the stage were very impressive. That’s about it. Once again, it was definitely the best concert I’ve ever been to.
