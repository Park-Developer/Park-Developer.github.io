XGA(Extended Graphic Array) Resolution

: XGA (Extended Graphics Array) resolution is ==1024 × 768 pixels== with a 4:3 aspect ratio. Originally introduced by IBM in 1990, it became a long-standing standard for computer displays, monitors, and business or classroom projectors
