Overview

: A codec(short for coder-decoder or compressor-decompressor) is a tool used to compress and decompress digital media files.

Codecs reduce file size while maintaining acceptable quality for storage or streaming. They operate by encoding raw video/audio data into a compressed format and decoding it during playback.
