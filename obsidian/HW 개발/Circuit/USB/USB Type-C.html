Pinmap

: USB Type-C Connector includes several new pins compared to USB Type-A and Type-B connectors.

These pons enable USB Type-C features such as higher power, Alternate Mode and reversibility.

  • TX/RX : SuperSpeed twisted pairs for USB 3.1 data(5 to 10 Gbps).
  • VBUS : Main System Bus(5~20V)

  • CC1/CC2(Configuration Channel) : CC lines used for cable detection, orientation and current advertisement.

    With USB PD, the CC lines can also communicate higher power levels and Alternate Mode.
