### My Pace

When I get nervous, I can’t hear other people's voices, so I sometimes miss what people say. So, I sometimes give the wrong answer. This tendency gives an unreliable impression to other people. 

Another problem is that I usually can’t perform at my best. I sometimes forget what I prepared. Sometimes, I realize myself that what I am saying is wrong. I want to proceed at my own pace and decrease communication risks. How can I do that?
